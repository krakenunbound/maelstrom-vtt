Everything below is committed on `main` (local commits, not pushed). Each entry
says what the app now does, the files, and the check that proves it. To see a
commit's whole change: `git show <commit>`. To list them all:
`git log --oneline 2dc19de6^..HEAD`.

The two largest are at the top: **games defined by their books** (a rules

## 2026-11-23

### 1. Games defined by their books (`game.json`) — commit 76b28118

A rules pack may carry a `game.json` at the top of its zip. Maelstrom reads it
when it reads the packs, registers the game, offers it on the campaign screen,
and a campaign of it sets up its sheet, character wizard, combat tracker and
party rolls from it. The numbers come from the books' records (each class's
`levels`, merged across books by level number; each monster's line; the
`Monster Attack Table` and `Normal Man` tables). No game's code: the core
reads the definition. Full format: `docs/pack-games.md`. D&D BECMI's
definition: `docs/games/becmi.game.json`; its records: `docs/becmi-packs.md`.

- **Definition reader**: `shared/packgame/definition.mjs` `readGame(raw)`.
  It fills the defaults (six abilities, the old modifier table, five saves,
  descending AC from 9, THAC0, d6 initiative by side, 2d6 morale, HP ability
  to 9th level, dead at 0, PP/GP/EP/SP/CP, `3d6*10` gold). A game's id is
  `pack<id>` (`packbecmi`): a character's `data.ruleset`.
- **Rules engine**: `shared/packgame/rules.mjs`, generic, given a `lib`
  `{ game, classes, items, tables }`:
  - `classesOf` merges class records by name;
  - `levelRow`/`levelValue` take the nearest level below that gives a value;
  - `derive(data, lib)` works out abilities and modifiers, HP (rolls + the
    ability to `abilityTo`, then the fixed `+N` of the level's `hitdice`), AC
    (armor `ac`, shield `acbonus`, Dex; descending or ascending), THAC0 or
    attack bonus, initiative, saves (the class row's, an ability's modifier
    where the save names one), attacks from ready weapons, skills, turning
    row, spells a day, load, class abilities by level, XP bonus and next level;
  - `testOf` (save at or above, ability check at or under, skill d100 at or
    under, loyalty 2d6 at or under);
  - `hitDiceOf` ("3*", "4+1", "1-1", "1/2");
  - `monsterThac0` (the books' Monster Attack Table by hit dice, else the
    definition's `monsterThac0`, else 19 - (HD - 1));
  - `savesAs` ("F2", "MU5", "NM" via the definition's `saveAs` codes, the class
    record's saves at that level, or the Normal Man table/definition);
  - `npcStats`, `hitNumber` (THAC0 - AC, or the AC);
  - `undeadKind`/`turnAgainst` (a number to roll, T, D, -);
  - `buildFromSpec` (scores, class with its `requirements`, first hit die, the
    shop in the game's coins, coins left), `levelUp`/`levelUpDie`.
- **Manifest from a definition**: `shared/packgame/manifest.mjs`
  `manifestOf(game)`: the sheet (core parts: abilities, AC, HP, initiative,
  load, saves, attacks, skills, spells, inventory, coins, notes), the
  declarative wizard (roll-stats, choose-record class, shop with gold, name,
  alignment with fixed choices; finish Ref `packgame.build`), tracker (saves
  keys, morale and turn-undead row actions), party (coins, group save and
  check), edit globs, `initiativeDie` (number of sides), Basic Fantasy's
  actions and bookkeeping specs reused.
- **Registration**: `shared/packgame/register.mjs` `registerPackGame(raw)` →
  `shared/rulesets/registry.mjs` new `registerGame(manifest)` (adds or replaces
  in `MANIFESTS`, `BY_NAME`, `BY_ID`, and the name into `CAMPAIGN_RULESETS`
  before Worldbuilding) and `packGames()` (the registered definitions).
  `RulesetManifest.packGame` added (`shared/rulesets/types.d.ts`).
- **Host**:
  - `electron/books.cjs` `describe()` reads `game.json` from a pack
    (`gameIn`, info `game`; `DESCRIBED` 6 → 7 so the cached index is read
    again); `playedName` makes a pack-defined game's books its own (not
    Worldbuilding's).
  - `electron/backend.cjs` `packGames()` registers every shelf book's game (at
    `pack-games` and before `start-host`); IPC `pack-games`
    (`electron/main.cjs` forward list, `electron/preload.cjs`
    `window.kraken.packGames`, `src/kraken.d.ts`).
  - `electron/tableServer.cjs`: the welcome (`type: "ok"`) carries `games`
    (`packGamesNow()`); `build-character` builds a pack game's character
    (`packGame.buildPg`).
  - `electron/packGame.cjs` (new): `gameNamed`, `gameOf`, `isPg`,
    `isPgRuleset`, `libOf(table, game)` (classes and items from the campaign,
    `table.bookTables()`), `buildPg` (the host rolls the first hit die),
    `checkPg`, `levelUpPg`, `editPg` (the manifest's edit globs).
  - `electron/siege.cjs`: check, level up, edit and drop-on-sheet (items to the
    inventory, a spell to the spellbook or prepared) route to `packGame`.
  - `electron/party.cjs` `giveItem`: a pack game's character.
  - `electron/agent/tools.cjs` `kraken_build_character`: a pack game.
  - `electron/combat.cjs`: `pg()`, `pgGame()` and an adapter `ob()` (Basic
    Fantasy's rules or the generic ones with the campaign's books). Basic
    Fantasy's weapon/attack/damage/save/morale/initiative/hp/thresholds paths
    serve pack games too; the attack hits on `THAC0 - AC` with descending AC
    (AC effects lower it) and the game's naturals; the chat line shows
    `[THAC0 n: NEEDS m]`; `turnUndeadPg` (the cleric's level row against each
    undead kind, the game's dice, T turned, D destroyed); the tracker view
    gives `turns`, `className`, `morale` and NPC attacks.
- **Renderer**: `src/setup/SetupScreen.tsx` registers the host's pack games
  and lists them; `src/session/useSession.ts` registers the welcome's `games`
  before setting the campaign's game; `src/rulesets/parts.tsx` the
  `packgame.build` wizard Ref; `src/core/wizard/Steps.tsx` a `text` part's
  `options.values` (fixed choices, e.g. alignments; type in
  `shared/rulesets/types.d.ts`).
- **Pack tools**: `scripts/kpack-make.mjs` puts a source folder's
  `game.json` in the zip (read with `readGame`; its `name` must equal the
  pack's `ruleset`); `scripts/kpack-check.mjs` checks it (now async) and no
  longer calls a game it does not know "shown in no campaign".
- **Check**: `scripts/check-pack-game.mjs` (`npm run pack-game`): a made-up
  game from packs only: registration, its books fit only its campaigns, a
  character built and raised into a second book's level, THAC0 attacks both
  ways, the Monster Attack Table, saves as a class, morale, turning, dead at 0.

  sheet's skill button shows `{shown}`.
- **Thrown weapons**: a ranged weapon that is not a bow, crossbow, sling or

- `kraken_build_character`'s description documents the pack-game spec.
